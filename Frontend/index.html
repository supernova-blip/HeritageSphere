About HeritageSphere

HeritageSphere is a digital heritage intelligence platform designed to organize, preserve and explore cultural heritage information.

The platform integrates structured, spatial, semi-structured, XML and graph-based heritage data to support research and historical analysis.

It combines PostgreSQL with PostGIS, MongoDB, Neo4j, XML technologies and a data warehouse to provide an integrated heritage information system.
